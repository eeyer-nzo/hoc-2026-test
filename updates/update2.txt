Title: Kongzi disappeared?
Date: 21 January 2024

<i>Written by Jason Chen</i>
In a mysterious incident, the Kongzi statue that stood at the center of the 正心园 disappeared overnight. Residents at the nearby condos reported a suspicious figure, donning a mask that resembled the face of Pritam Singh sneaking into the Dunman High School compound to dismantle the statue. 

Reports have been made that the kongzi statue was found on the inner grounds of the Istana, near the Prime Minister's office, but the Traits Times cannot officially confirm this sighting as of yet.
